Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This is a simple overview of how Quinton DEV handles information for its services, including Dev Connect.

Information we handle

Depending on the service you use, we may handle information such as your name, email address, account identifiers, and authentication information. When you sign in with Google, we may receive your Google account name, email address, email verification status, and unique Google account identifier.

Why we use it

We use this information to provide authentication, create and maintain your account, provide the features of our services, and keep those services working.

Google user data

Google user data is used only for the functionality described above. We do not sell Google user data and do not use it for advertising. We only request information needed for the service to work.

Sharing and storage

Information may be processed by service providers we use to operate our applications and infrastructure, such as hosting, database, authentication, and cloud service providers. We do not sell your personal information.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information we hold. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain accounts, meet operational requirements, or comply with legal obligations.

Your rights

Depending on your circumstances and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. To ask a privacy question or make a request, contact Quinton DEV.

Changes

This policy may be updated as our services develop. The latest version will always be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Quinton DEV through the contact details provided on our website.
