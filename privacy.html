DEV CONNECT / QUINTON DEV

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This policy explains what information Dev Connect handles, why we need it, where it is stored, and how we use it. Dev Connect is an independent software project operated by Quinton DEV.

What information does Dev Connect collect?

We try not to collect information simply because we can. We collect or receive information when it is required to provide a feature.

For an account, this can include your name, email address and internal account information. If you choose Google Sign-In, Google provides us with information from your Google account that is needed to authenticate you. This can include:

  • Your Google account name
  • Your Google account email address
  • Whether Google has verified the email address
  • Your unique Google account identifier (the sub value)

We do not receive or store your Google password.

Why do we use Google account information?

Google account information is used to sign you into Dev Connect and associate your Google account with your Dev Connect account. The unique Google identifier allows us to recognise the same Google account when you sign in again.

Your name and email address may be used as part of your Dev Connect account information and to provide account-related functionality.

How we use your information

We use information we handle to:

  • Authenticate you and maintain your Dev Connect account
  • Associate your account with the identity you use to sign in
  • Provide and operate Dev Connect features
  • Keep our API and supporting services working and secure
  • Respond to support requests where you contact us
  • Meet legal or operational requirements where applicable

We do not sell personal information. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not use Google user data for unrelated purposes.

How Google user data is handled

Dev Connect's use of Google user data is limited to providing the functionality described above. We request only the Google account information needed for authentication and account management.

Google user data is not sold or transferred to data brokers or used to build advertising profiles. We only share information with service providers when that processing is necessary to operate the service, as described below.

Where is information stored?

Dev Connect uses Supabase for database infrastructure. Account information and application data stored by Dev Connect may therefore be stored and processed using Supabase's infrastructure.

We use Supabase because we would rather use established infrastructure for things like database storage than pretend a small developer project needs to run its own data centre. Supabase provides encryption at rest for stored data, and our application communicates with its services over encrypted connections.

Our API and other application components may also run on cloud infrastructure used to operate Dev Connect.

Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect information used by Dev Connect. Connections to our services are made over HTTPS/TLS, and stored database data is protected by the encryption provided by our infrastructure providers.

Authentication is handled through OAuth providers such as Google rather than asking us to store your Google password. Access to application infrastructure is restricted to the credentials and permissions required to operate it.

No internet-connected service can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we aim to use sensible security practices and avoid collecting information that we do not need.

Who do we share information with?

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure and service providers that are necessary to run Dev Connect, including:

  • Google, when you choose Google Sign-In, to authenticate your Google account
  • Supabase, for database infrastructure used by Dev Connect
  • Other hosting or infrastructure providers where required to operate our API and application

We do not share Google user data with third parties for advertising or unrelated commercial purposes.

How long do we keep information?

We keep account information for as long as your Dev Connect account is active or while it is reasonably required to provide and maintain the service. If information is no longer needed, we aim to delete it or allow it to be deleted where appropriate.

Some information may need to be retained for longer where necessary for security, fraud prevention, backups, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live and the law that applies to you, you may have rights over your personal information, including the right to request access, correction or deletion.

If you want to ask what information we hold about you, request a correction, request deletion, or ask a privacy question, contact us at support@quintondev.com.

Children

Dev Connect is a developer-focused service and is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where doing so would be unlawful.

Changes to this policy

Dev Connect is an active development project, so the way the service works may change. If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this policy and publish the latest version on this page.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests can be sent to support@quintondev.com.
