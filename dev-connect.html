Dev Connect

A small developer-focused application built to make connecting with and managing developer projects a little easier.

What is it?

Dev Connect is one of Quinton DEV's projects. It provides a mobile application and supporting services for developer-related functionality.

Google Sign-In

Dev Connect supports signing in with Google. When you use Google Sign-In, the service receives the information required to identify your Google account and associate it with your Dev Connect account.

For more information about how information is handled, see our Privacy Policy.
